The Science of Smooth Texture

At The Velvet Churn, we strictly monitor our manufacturing parameters to create an exceptionally rich dessert matrix. By enforcing a dense 14% to 16% butterfat composition and keeping air incorporation (overrun) below 40%, we completely eliminate coarse water crystals. This gives our ice cream its signature velvety mouthfeel and remarkably slow-melting structure.
